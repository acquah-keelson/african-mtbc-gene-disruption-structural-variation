## Interpretation

The gene-state counts agree with the published aggregate
disruption totals.

Variant-positive counts and final gene-state classifications
are distinct analytical measures.

The differences observed for htpG and fadD15 require
isolate-level variant-to-state matching before attributing
specific variants to particular gene-state classifications.

This audit verifies aggregate gene-state counts but does not
independently validate nucleotide substitutions, codon
consequences, or isolate-level variant concordance.
