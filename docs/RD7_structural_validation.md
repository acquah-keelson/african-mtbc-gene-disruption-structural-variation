# RD7/mce3 structural-variation verification

## Objective

This report documents computational verification of the
RD7/mce3-associated structural interval in the existing
African MTBC genomic dataset.

No new sequencing, alignment or variant calling was performed.

## Evidence sources

The verification uses archived outputs from:

- 12C regional CIGAR-aware interval audit.
- 12D sample-level explicit deletion audit.
- 12D deletion-boundary summary.
- RD7 structural-support figure source data.

## Lineage 6 findings

The assembly-based analysis included 75 Lineage 6 genomes.

All 75 were classified as:

near_absent_limited_flank_support

The explicit-deletion audit reported that all 75 genomes
contained a deletion spanning the assessed RD7-associated
interval.

The recurrent inferred deletion coordinates were:

H37Rv: 2,208,006–2,220,724

Reported deletion length: 12,719 bp.

## Lineage 4 comparison

The comparison included 125 Lineage 4 assemblies.

Of these, 117 were classified as near_complete_continuous
and eight as near_complete_fragmented.

Five assemblies had small deletion operations overlapping
the region, but none contained the entire assessed interval.

## Interpretation

The archived alignment-based computational evidence
supports a large RD7/mce3-associated deletion across
the 75 assessed Lineage 6 assemblies.

This evidence is consistent with the lineage-associated
gene-state findings from the larger cohort.

The analysis does not establish independently validated
nucleotide-resolution deletion junctions.

The 75 assemblies are a selected subset of the original
Lineage 6 cohort, not an independent validation population.

## Remaining limitation

The relationship between the smaller assessed core interval
and the larger 12,719-bp inferred deletion requires explicit
coordinate clarification in the manuscript.

These results should not be interpreted as population-wide
deletion prevalence or as evidence of a measured
functional phenotype.
